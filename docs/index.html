How It Works

A scenario engine that grades its own homework.

Click any stage below to see both the everyday explanation and the exact statistical machinery behind Outlook.

1. Look only at what was actually known on that day

Before estimating a range, Tenerus gathers up to 10 years of completed daily prices and volume, checks how the ticker is moving relative to the S&P 500, and reads any headline tone saved on or before that date. Future closes are completely invisible—even during historical replays.

Under the hood

10 strictly causal features (5d/20d/60d momentum, 20d volatility, 5d/20d vol ratio, 60d drawdown, volume/range ratios, ^GSPC relative strength & market vol, and 14-day decayed sentiment from sentiment-history.json).

Engineering in Plain Language

Serious statistics, explained without jargon.

Every choice under the hood exists for one reason: to prevent wishful thinking, hindsight bias, and overconfident forecasts.

GARCH(1,1) + Cornish-Fisher

Storms pass—volatility shouldn't stay stuck on high.

Naive volatility formulas assume a wild week will last all quarter. Tenerus uses a stationary mean-reverting volatility model so 5-session bands respect today's turbulence while 60-session bands calm back toward normal levels, tilted for return asymmetry.

Split-Conformal Calibration

A built-in honesty meter for uncertainty ranges.

If an "80% range" only caught 65% of actual closes on past dates, it was too narrow. Tenerus measures how far outside past misses landed and automatically widens (or tightens) the live interval without assuming markets follow a textbook bell curve.

Walk-Forward News Ablation

Prove headlines help—don't just assume they do.

Tenerus scores headlines locally with zero external APIs, saves daily tone to sentiment-history.json, and runs a clean A/B check on past dates comparing its quantile model with vs. without news—reporting honestly if headlines helped or just added noise.

Brier Score, ECE & ^GSPC Alpha

Did an AI beat a coin flip—or the S&P 500?

Claiming "90% confidence" only matters if those calls are right ~90% of the time, and a +10% stock pick isn't skill if the S&P 500 gained +15%. The Prediction Logbook measures calibration error and excess return over the exact same dates.

Opt-In TimesFM & Chronos-2

Race local foundation models head-to-head.

If you install Google TimesFM or Amazon Chronos-2 in your own Python environment, Tenerus can run 12 past cutoffs plus today in a single load, apply conformal calibration, overlay the band on your chart, and show a direct scoreboard against Outlook.

Native Rust · 137 Automated Tests

Fast startup, tight security, zero middlemen.

Built in Rust with egui. API keys never touch plaintext settings files or process arguments (piped via stdin to OS-trusted curl), remote calls enforce HTTPS, and automated tests prove mutating future prices never leaks into past replays.

What actually self-calibrates

  • Outlook 4-Model Ensemble: Online quantile weights, Hedge model weights, and split-conformal intervals update causally as new daily closes arrive.
  • Local TimesFM / Chronos-2 Pilots: Split-conformal calibration adjusts foundation model bands from strictly prior walk-forward errors.

What is prompt context (not retraining)

  • BYO AI Research & CLI Briefings: Reviewed logbook outcomes and calibration scores are supplied in the prompt so the LLM can temper overconfidence—no LLM weights are ever fine-tuned.
  • No hidden feedback loops: Every input, weight, and benchmark comparison stays inspectable on screen and in exported reports.
